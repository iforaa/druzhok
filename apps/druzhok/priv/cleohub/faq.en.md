# What I can do

- Answer questions, help with texts, emails, translations and ideas.
- Search the web and read pages from a link.
- Understand voice messages and reply by voice.
- See photos and make sense of them: text, receipts, screenshots.
- Read documents: PDF, Word, Excel, presentations. I can make such a file too.
- Set reminders and run tasks on a schedule.
- Remember what you ask me to remember.

- Draw pictures from a description and edit a picture you send.

- Make simple websites, if this is on in «Cabinet» → Settings.
- Change my own avatar from your description (up to 3 times a day).

# What I can't do

- I don't log into your accounts, email or bank, and I don't pay or buy anything.
- I don't call or message other people on my own.
- In a group I only search the web: no files, reminders or memory there.
- I don't change the plan or take payments: that is in «Cabinet».

# Common questions

**Where are the plan and payments?**
In «Cabinet»: the menu button next to the message field. It has the plan, limits, payment, settings and support.

**What are the 5-hour and weekly limits?**
On some plans, work is counted per 5 hours and per week. When a limit is used up, I answer again once it resets or after a plan change. Ask me and I'll tell you how much is used and when it resets.

**How do I start a fresh conversation?**
Send /new. What I remember about you stays.

**How do I add you to a group?**
Add me to the group and approve it in «Cabinet»; until then I stay silent there. How I answer in groups is also set in «Cabinet» → Settings.

**How do I invite a friend?**
Ask me for a link for a friend: with it they can create their own bot.

**Something doesn't work. What do I do?**
Ask me whether there is an outage right now. If not, write to «Cabinet» → Support.
